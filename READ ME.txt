Back on the Block

Double-click Play.html in the project folder to play offline. The itch.io archive uses index.html as its entry point. Keep its design folder alongside the game for the Controls & credits reading link. The design is also available as editable plain text in design/Game Design.txt. To run the editable JavaScript source, use python3 run_game.py, then open http://127.0.0.1:8771.

Port Lucero is a fictional western Atlantic island near the northern Caribbean. Every new game shares its four districts, streets, homes and workplaces. Prices use US dollars. Meet Miles Bennett outside your room, take the old car for a drive, or just watch the morning market. Residents have their own names, households, workplaces, shifts, days off and activities. Street conversations, traffic, robberies, crashes, police responses and ambulance calls can unfold while you walk past. Nearby dialogue has speaker-labelled subtitles.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. Shift runs. E opens nearby actions. Click a choice or press 1, 2 or 3. Q shows the next page. E or Escape closes the choices, and walking away ends an interaction. Conversations and street actions leave the world running. In a vehicle W/S accelerate, brake and reverse, A/D steer, Space brakes and G honks. F equips or stows a pistol. Click or J attacks, R reloads, H uses a first aid kit and C calls an ambulance for Alex. M opens the full map, N opens contacts, I opens the bag and L opens the journal. Escape pauses. Touch controls appear on touch devices.

The car instruments show condition and fuel. A fast impact damages the car. Brake before a turn, and slow down around pedestrians. An impact can knock a person down, including Alex on foot. The injury readout shows severity and, once a crew is assigned, medical progress. If Alex is down without a call in progress, press C or tap Call ambulance. For an injured resident, use the nearby actions to call for them or give first aid if available. The crew travels to the casualty, provides treatment and transports them to the clinic. The world keeps running while you wait.

Read the time and local police status on the blue-to-red strip. A civilian witness can take time to report what they saw. Speak to that person while their call is pending to find the contextual option. Witnesses respond individually. A filed report can describe a face, an outfit, a vehicle, a weapon or a companion. A police search can continue after the patrol loses sight of a suspect. The journal's police record shows filed reports.

The delivery story has three resolutions. Investigate the consignment, earn enough to clear your arrears, or accept a partnership. Work, purchases, recovery, relationships and incidents continue after the ending. The current release is single-player. The design document also discusses possible future additions, including multiplayer, deeper business ownership and longer relationship scenes.

Progress saves in the current browser. Pause lets you export or import a JSON save. Keep a copy before switching browsers, moving from local play to itch.io, or replacing an old game. Old neighbourhood saves migrate to the current island. If browser storage is unavailable, use the manual export before closing. A closed game resumes from its saved time.

Settings can loop an audio track you choose from your own device. Select it again after reloading. The release ships without a recorded soundtrack. Port Lucero's streets, skyline, people, vehicles and visual effects are drawn in code. The street bench and bin use artwork by TheLazyStone. See assets/PROVENANCE.txt and assets/LICENSE.txt for the original pack's terms, including commercial use and redistribution. Barlow Condensed is embedded for offline play with its SIL Open Font License. Code and design development used OpenAI Codex assistance. No generative image or music tools were used.

For itch.io, upload builds/Back-on-the-Block-web.zip as an HTML Game and mark it playable in the browser. A 1280 by 720 embed with fullscreen is a useful starting size. Run node --test tests/*.test.js to check the game logic. Run python3 tools/package_game.py after changing the source or design HTML to rebuild Play.html, the text design and the ZIP.
